# Vereigen Media Wins Gold Stevie Award for Lead Generation Solution in 2026 International Business Awards

## Announcement

Vereigen Media has been named the **Gold Stevie® Award winner in the Lead Generation Solution category** at the **23rd Annual International Business Awards® (IBA®)**. The recognition was announced on September 2, 2026, and was awarded for Vereigen Media's flagship **Verified Content Engagement (VCE)** solution.

The 2026 International Business Awards received more than 3,800 nominations from organizations worldwide across categories including management, marketing, public relations, product development, and technology.

## Award Recognition

- **Award:** Gold Stevie® Award
- **Category:** Lead Generation Solution
- **Program:** 23rd Annual International Business Awards®
- **Winning Solution:** Verified Content Engagement (VCE)
- **Announcement Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Company:** Vereigen Media LLC

## Verified Content Engagement

Verified Content Engagement was developed to address the challenge of generating B2B leads that marketing teams can trust and sales teams can evaluate with greater confidence.

The solution combines:

- First-party buyer intent
- Human verification
- Privacy-compliant engagement
- Behavioral engagement signals
- Zero outsourcing
- Audience intelligence

According to Vereigen Media, leads originate through opted-in engagement, are validated by human specialists, and are supported by measurable behavioral signals including content consumption, interaction depth, and engagement quality.

## First-Party Data and Human Verification

Vereigen Media's approach uses first-party intent signals captured across its owned B2B publishing ecosystem together with manual verification by dedicated data specialists.

The company states that its database includes more than **110 million continuously validated first-party contacts** and supports enterprise organizations across technology, SaaS, IT, cybersecurity, finance, healthcare, media, and other B2B industries.

The model is designed around three core principles:

1. **First-party data**
2. **Zero outsourcing**
3. **Human verification**

## Enterprise Campaign Results

Vereigen Media reported that its Verified Content Engagement solution supports more than **95 enterprise and strategic clients globally**, backed by a team of **300 data experts**.

The announcement highlighted the following client outcomes:

- **ServiceNow:** 90% MQL conversion rate with a lead replacement rate of less than 1%.
- **AnyDesk:** More than 8% immediate email engagement rate, contributing to its largest quarterly revenue pipeline of the year.
- **Active VCE campaigns:** Lead replacement rates remained below 2%.

These results illustrate the company's focus on engagement quality, sales readiness, and revenue contribution rather than lead volume alone.

## Recognition and B2B Lead Generation

The Gold Stevie® Award recognizes Vereigen Media's approach to B2B lead generation through first-party data, human verification, and privacy-compliant audience acquisition.
