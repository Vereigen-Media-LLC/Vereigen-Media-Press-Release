# Vereigen Media Named to the 2026 Inc. 5000 List of America’s Fastest-Growing Private Companies

## About the Announcement

Vereigen Media has been named to the **2026 Inc. 5000 list of America’s Fastest-Growing Private Companies**, ranking **No. 648 nationally**.

The recognition highlights the company's sustained growth and its continued focus on data accuracy, compliance, and a zero-outsourcing approach in B2B demand generation.

## Key Rankings

- **No. 648** nationally on the 2026 Inc. 5000
- **Top 13%** of companies on the national list
- **No. 27** in the Austin–Round Rock–San Marcos, Texas, metro area
- **No. 48** in the Advertising, Marketing & PR sector nationwide
- **No. 78** among companies based in Texas

## What the Recognition Reflects

The recognition follows several years of sustained growth as Vereigen Media supports organizations across technology, SaaS, cybersecurity, cloud, IT, and other complex B2B industries.

Vereigen Media's approach to B2B demand generation is built around first-party, opted-in lead engagement, with an emphasis on data quality and accountable audience engagement.

Two core principles support this approach:

1. **Zero outsourcing for data collection and verification**
2. **Human verification before each lead reaches the client**

## Focus on Data Quality and Verified Engagement

Vereigen Media prioritizes data quality, compliance, and verified engagement as part of its B2B demand generation model.

The company helps organizations build stronger marketing and sales pipelines through first-party, opted-in lead engagement while maintaining a focus on accurate and accountable data.

## About Vereigen Media

Vereigen Media is a B2B demand generation company focused on first-party data intelligence and human-verified engagement.

Its solutions include:

- Account-Based Marketing
- Demand Generation
- Verified Content Engagement
- VM Engage
- Event Registration
